# JobShield AI methodology

## Decision model
JobShield separates source evidence from interpretation.

1. Preserve the original recruitment message.
2. Extract candidate evidence snippets.
3. Require human confirmation.
4. Apply deterministic rules to confirmed evidence.
5. Present findings as NOTICE, MEANING, ACTION.
6. Preserve uncertainty and recommend independent verification.
7. Export a case record.

## Non-negotiable controls
- No unsupported allegation of fraud.
- No invented payment request.
- No model-generated final concern level.
- A company-record match is not proof of recruiter authority or offer authenticity.
- Human review precedes findings.

## Demo case JS-001
BrightPath is fictional. The sample contains time pressure, a pay claim and WhatsApp recruitment, while explicitly saying no payment is required. The system therefore demonstrates both warning detection and a negative constraint: it must not manufacture a payment request.
